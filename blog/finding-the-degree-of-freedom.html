Find the Overlooked Degree of Freedom

Different problems. One repeatable process.

A disciplined approach to identifying the real constraint, finding exploitable design space, and selecting the simplest architecture that produces measurable product value.

01 — Analyze Fundamentals

Start from device, circuit, timing, and system behavior.

02 — Find the Degree of Freedom

Map the design space and identify relationships conventional optimization has overlooked.

03 — Choose the Simplest Architecture

Add complexity only when the additional complexity creates measurable product value.

04 — Verify on Silicon

Validate timing, power, margin, variation, and operating behavior under implementation conditions.

05 — Productize and Scale

Convert solutions into constraints, guidelines, evaluation frameworks, and architecture baselines.

Find the overlooked degree of freedom, validate it on silicon, and leave behind a stronger architecture for the next design.
